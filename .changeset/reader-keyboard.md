An open conversation can now be read with the keyboard: press → or Enter (or click it), then ↑ and ↓ move between its emails and the reply you're writing, → and ← open and close an email, → or Enter on the reply goes back into its text, and Esc or ← goes back to the list. Esc in a reply now steps out to the conversation first.
