The mail database on your computer is encrypted, with its key kept in your system keychain. A database from an earlier version is encrypted the first time this version starts with no other copy of the app open; quit any older copy before updating, as it can't read the encrypted database. If the keychain doesn't answer, the app waits for it with Try again and Quit; if the key is missing or doesn't match, the app starts a new cache and asks you to sign in again.
