Templates: save emails you write often and start from them, from the Templates button while writing (⇧⌘T) or from ⌘K. A template can set From, the people it's to and the subject, and can have fields that you fill in each time it's used: text, a list of items, a date or a date and time in the format you choose, a choice, or a first name. Make and edit them in Settings → Templates, or save what you're writing as one. They're kept on this computer, for all your accounts.
