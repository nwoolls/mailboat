The app runs on Linux, as a `.deb`, an `.rpm` or an AppImage (x86-64 and Arm), and updates itself there as on the Mac and Windows. Clicking a notification opens its conversation; the `.deb` and `.rpm` can be the default email app; and ChatGPT runs fenced in with bubblewrap where it's installed and allowed. Signing in needs the desktop's keyring, such as GNOME Keyring or KWallet, and Settings has no Transparency there, since the window isn't see-through. Opening the app again while it's open asks its window to come forward, on Windows too, and on Windows "Show in Finder" now reads "Show in File Explorer".
