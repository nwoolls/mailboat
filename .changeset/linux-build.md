The app runs on Linux, as a `.deb`, an `.rpm` or an AppImage (x86-64 and Arm), and updates itself there as on the Mac and Windows. It follows the desktop's light or dark theme. On desktops that pass a click on (GNOME, KDE and most others), clicking a notification opens its conversation. The `.deb` and `.rpm` can be the default email app, and ChatGPT runs fenced in with bubblewrap where it's installed and allowed. Signing in needs the desktop's keyring, such as GNOME Keyring or KWallet. Settings has no Transparency on Linux, since the window isn't see-through, and a saved file is shown with "Show in Folder". Opening the app again while it's open asks its window to come forward, on Windows too. On Windows, "Show in Finder" now reads "Show in File Explorer", the theme setting names Windows, and Settings no longer shows the notification style button, which only works on the Mac.
